REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL (RFP)
==========================================================

Client: Rajasthan State Power Distribution Corporation Ltd (RSPDC)
Project: 33kV Substation Upgrade & Feeder Cable Replacement
RFP Reference: RSPDC/ELEC/2025-26/PO-4487
Date of Issue: 30 May 2025
Submission Deadline: 15 June 2025
Delivery Location: Jaipur Central Substation, Mansarovar, Rajasthan

==========================================================
SCOPE OF WORK
==========================================================

RSPDC invites sealed bids from qualified cable manufacturers
for the supply of power cables for the 33kV substation
modernisation project. The scope includes low-voltage internal
wiring, medium-voltage feeder cables, and high-voltage
underground transmission cables.

==========================================================
TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS — LINE ITEMS
==========================================================

LINE ITEM 1: Internal Panel Wiring
  - 3 core, 4 sq.mm, Copper conductor, XLPE insulated
  - 1100V grade, quantity: 2000 meters
  - Application: LT panel internal connections

LINE ITEM 2: LT Distribution Feeder Cable
  - 4 core, 25 sq.mm, Copper conductor, PVC insulated
  - 1100V grade, SWA (Steel Wire Armoured), quantity: 3500 meters
  - Application: Underground LT distribution from transformer

LINE ITEM 3: MV Feeder Cable (CUSTOM REQUIREMENT)
  - 3 core, 120 sq.mm, Copper conductor, EPR insulated
  - 3300V grade, SWA armoured, quantity: 1500 meters
  - Application: Medium voltage feeder from 33/11kV transformer
  - Must comply with IS 7098 Part 2 for MV cables

LINE ITEM 4: HV Underground Transmission Cable (CUSTOM REQUIREMENT)
  - 1 core, 400 sq.mm, Aluminium conductor, XLPE insulated
  - 11000V (11kV) grade, quantity: 800 meters
  - Application: HV underground transmission from grid interconnect
  - Must comply with IEC 60502-2 and IS 7098 Part 2 for HT cables
  - Requires partial discharge testing at 2.5 U0

LINE ITEM 5: Control & Instrumentation Cable (CUSTOM REQUIREMENT)
  - 12 core, 1.5 sq.mm, Copper conductor, LSZH insulated
  - 600V grade, overall screened with aluminium-mylar tape
  - Quantity: 1200 meters
  - Application: SCADA control signalling between relay panels
  - Must be fire-retardant per IEC 60332-3 Category C

LINE ITEM 6: Aluminium Feeder Cable
  - 3 core, 240 sq.mm, Aluminium conductor, XLPE insulated
  - 1100V grade, SWA armoured, quantity: 4000 meters
  - Application: Main LT distribution backbone

==========================================================
COMMERCIAL TERMS
==========================================================
- Payment: 30% advance, 60% on delivery, 10% after commissioning
- Warranty: 5 years from date of commissioning
- Liquidated Damages: 0.5% per week of delay, max 10%
- Delivery: Within 8 weeks of purchase order
- Inspection: Third-party inspection at factory by RSPDC nominee

==========================================================
COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENTS
==========================================================
- All cables must comply with relevant IS/IEC standards
- Type test reports from NABL accredited laboratory
- Manufacturer must hold valid ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 14001
- BIS license for relevant cable types mandatory
